Common Questions

FAQ

Can I get a free trial before joining?
Yes! We offer a complimentary trial session for all new members. Just WhatsApp or call us to schedule a slot. No commitment required — come in, train, and see if it's the right fit.
I'm a complete beginner. Is this gym for me?
Absolutely. Many of our current members had zero gym experience when they started. Coach Sameer will assess your starting point and build a beginner-friendly program that progresses at the right pace.
Do you offer personal training sessions?
Yes, one-on-one personal training is available. Sessions are scheduled around your availability and Coach Sameer personally designs and adjusts your program throughout.
Is the gym air-conditioned?
The entire facility is fully air-conditioned, which matters a lot in Goa's climate. You can train comfortably at any time of day without the heat affecting your performance.
Do you provide diet and nutrition advice?
Basic nutrition guidance and meal structuring is included with personal training plans. For more detailed dietary programs, ask Coach Sameer during your trial session.
Is there parking available near the studio?
We're located near Mapusa Market, where ample street parking is available. Two-wheelers can be parked right outside. Getting here is easy from anywhere in North Goa.
Can women join Power Studio?
Yes, Power Studio is fully open to all genders. We have a welcoming, no-judgement environment and several female members who train here regularly.
Can I pause my membership if I travel?
We understand Goa residents and expats travel often. Speak to us about your situation — we handle freezes and pauses on a case-by-case basis and will work something out.
